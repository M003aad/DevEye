# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to a welcoming and respectful community. This policy is adapted from [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include being considerate, communicating constructively, accepting respectful feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, publishing another person's private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project. Project maintainers may remove or edit contributions and may restrict participation when needed to address unacceptable behavior.
